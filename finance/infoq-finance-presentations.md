# Applied Finance: InfoQ Conference Presentations

Long-form conference talks for studying corporate finance, managerial economics, accounting, investment decisions and business profitability in practical contexts. All links point to individual InfoQ presentation pages.

**Priority:** ★★★ essential; ★★ substantial financial content; ★ useful supplementary material.
