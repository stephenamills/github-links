- **OpEx (operating expenditure):** Generally recognized as an expense when incurred or consumed.
- **CapEx (capital expenditure):** Generally recorded as an asset and expensed over its useful life through depreciation or amortization.

Older accounting resources are useful conceptually, but their specific accounting and tax rules should not be assumed current.

A company can be pleased that its cloud bill is increasing if revenue is growing sufficiently relative to direct costs.

| Metric | Before growth | After growth |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Monthly revenue | $100,000 | $200,000 |
| Direct service costs | $30,000 | $45,000 |
| Gross profit | $70,000 | $155,000 |
| Gross margin | 70% | 77.5% |

Costs increased, but the business became more profitable and economically efficient.
